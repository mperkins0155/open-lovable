My Wedding Vows
✦

Allyse,

You are my penguin — I chose you. I will never choose another.

You not only make me laugh and smile, but you bring a kind of happiness into my life that I didn't know was possible. Every day with you feels like something I used to only hope for.

I promise to stand beside you and to show you — not just tell you — how much I love and appreciate you. Words are easy. I intend to make sure you feel it.

I ask one thing of you in return: be open with me. When I fall short — and I will, because I am human — tell me. I need to know, because you are the most important person in my life, and I never want comfort or complacency to make me forget that.

I cannot promise you perfection. When we are upset, when life is hard, we sometimes lose sight of what matters most.

But I can promise you this:

I will always come back.
I will always choose to do better.

Because you are worth every effort I have to give — now, and always.

With all my love
Michael Perkins
